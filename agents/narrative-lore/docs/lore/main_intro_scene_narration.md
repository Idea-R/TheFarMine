### Main Intro Scene Narrative

#### Overview
The dwarves, once rulers of expansive mountain kingdoms, have been forced deeper into the earth due to mysterious tremors and surface conflicts. Now, they journey into 'The Far Mine,' seeking refuge and resources.

#### Key Characters
- **Thrain Ironheart**: The stoic leader of the expedition, known for his wisdom and tactical mind.
- **Bruni Boulderfist**: A fearless warrior and the shield of the people, with a heart as unyielding as the deepest stone.
- **Drissa Oreweaver**: A master engineer, whose inventions pivot on the edge of impossible.

#### Historical Context
Centuries ago, the dwarven kingdoms flourished on the surface, tapping into the riches of the mountains. However, a sequence of devastating tremors, accompanied by aggressive incursions from rival species and the awakening of slumbering mountain beasts, drove the clans to seek shelter in the forgotten depths.

Their exodus into the underdark leads them to The Far Mine—a place of ancient lore said to harbor untold resources and, possibly, a new beginning.
