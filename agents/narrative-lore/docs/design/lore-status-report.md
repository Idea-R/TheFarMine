## Top 3 Priorities

### 1. Create the Lore Bible

**Why:** Every piece of content must reference a canonical source. Without it, inconsistencies will compound as the project grows.

**Deliverable:** `docs/lore/lore-bible.md` containing:
- World overview and tone guidelines
- Canonical terminology (The Far Mine, underdark, steam technology)
- Core lore pillars (Valor, Duty, Innovation)
- List of canonical characters, factions, and locations

---

### 2. Expand Character Profiles

**Why:** Thrain, Bruni, and Drissa are introduced but not fleshed out. Players need to connect with these characters through backstory and motivation.

**Deliverable:** `docs/lore/characters/` directory with individual profiles including:
- Full backstory and personal history
- Motivations and goals
- Relationships with other characters
- Voice and personality notes for dialogue consistency

---

### 3. Define the Great Cog Council & Guild Structure

**Why:** The political and social structure drives internal conflict and quest design. Factions create narrative tension.

**Deliverable:** `docs/lore/factions/great-cog-council.md` covering:
- Council composition (Mining Guild, Engineering Guild, Cogwork Guild)
- Power dynamics and rivalries
- Key NPCs within each guild
- Potential quest hooks tied to faction politics

---

## Conclusion

The Far Mine has a strong narrative foundation in the intro scene, but the lore remains largely conceptual. Establishing the Lore Bible as the canonical reference is the critical first step — all other content flows from it.
